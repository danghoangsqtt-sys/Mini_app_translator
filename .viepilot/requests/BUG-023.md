# BUG: Bluetooth capability gate falsely reports BLE unavailable and blocks pairing

## Meta
- **ID**: BUG-023
- **Type**: Bug
- **Status**: in_progress
- **Priority**: High
- **Created**: 2026-09-23
- **Reporter**: Product owner physical-device test
- **Assignee**: TERRA
- **Phase**: Phase 9, Task 9.1

## Summary

The app treats `BluetoothAdapter.isMultipleAdvertisementSupported()` as equivalent to general Bluetooth Low Energy support through `BluetoothCommunicator 1.0.6`. Some valid phones therefore receive an inaccurate “device does not have Bluetooth Low Energy” message and pairing is blocked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pairing capability is evaluated from the actual operations required by the app, not only multiple-advertisement support.
- [ ] A phone without multiple advertisement support is not described as lacking BLE.
- [ ] Unsupported states are non-fatal and explain the unavailable capability accurately.
- [ ] Permission denial, Bluetooth disabled, unsupported hardware, and library failure remain distinguishable.
- [ ] Focused unit/source tests prevent restoration of the old unconditional gate.
- [ ] Pixel emulator and at least one physical phone complete launch and pairing-screen smoke tests.
