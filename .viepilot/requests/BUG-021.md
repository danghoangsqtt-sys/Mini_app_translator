## Summary
The English and Italian onboarding notices claim that Mini Conversation is free for the first `$300` spent during the first year after creating a Google Cloud account. The current Google Cloud free trial is `$300` for 90 days, while Cloud Translation and Speech-to-Text have separate service-specific pricing. The app therefore presents outdated, financially relevant setup guidance.
