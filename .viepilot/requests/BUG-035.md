## Summary

The transport ingress and recent-peer persistence work are now bounded, but three release-reachable queues remain unbounded: pending Conversation translations, outbound Bluetooth message/data queues, and the voice-service UI history returned as one Binder `Bundle`. A long or adversarial session can grow memory until the process is killed, or make restore fail with `TransactionTooLargeException`.

## Evidence

- `ConversationOnDeviceController.incoming` is an unbounded `ArrayDeque`; every decoded peer payload is appended while only one translation is processed at a time.
- `BluetoothCommunicator.pendingMessages` and `pendingData` are unbounded `ArrayDeque` instances; writes append indefinitely when channels stall or callbacks never complete.
- `VoiceTranslationService.messages` retains every `GuiMessage` for the service lifetime, and `GET_ATTRIBUTES` sends the entire `ArrayList` through one Messenger/Binder `Bundle`.
- The manifest enables `android:largeHeap="true"`, which delays rather than removes the failure and increases the amount of state that can later be marshalled.

Android documents a process-wide Binder transaction buffer of about 1 MB and recommends keeping transactions small; oversized replies can throw `TransactionTooLargeException` with partial-failure semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Incoming translation and outbound message/data queues have explicit count and byte budgets with documented overflow/backpressure behavior.
- [ ] Queue limits cannot silently discard control frames or corrupt Conversation framing; overflow is sanitized and recoverable.
- [ ] Voice history has an explicit bounded retention policy and never returns the complete unbounded session in one Binder transaction.
- [ ] Attribute restore uses paging/chunking or a small snapshot with a tested byte ceiling well below the Binder process limit.
- [ ] Stress tests cover slow/stalled translation, stalled channels, long text sessions, reconnect, and restore near every configured bound.
- [ ] Memory/queue telemetry contains counts and byte totals only, never conversation content or peer identifiers.
